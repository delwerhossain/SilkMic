The first release of SilkMic: free, on-device AI noise cancellation and dictation for Mac.

## What's in 1.0.0

- **Clean Mic everywhere**: AI noise removal (DeepFilterNet3) on the "SilkMic" virtual microphone for Zoom, Meet, Teams, Discord, WhatsApp and every other app. Light, Balanced, Strong and Max levels.
- **Any mic**: built-in, USB and Bluetooth (AirPods and others), with automatic recovery when Bluetooth switches to call mode.
- **Notch panel**: hover the MacBook notch to switch microphone and speakers and control Clean Mic.
- **Dictation**: hold Fn (Globe), speak, release, and the text is typed into any app. English on-device; optional Bangla (Bengali script) model.
- **Per-app volume mixer**, device connect/disconnect alerts, launch at login.

## Install

1. Download **SilkMic-1.0.0.dmg** below, open it and drag SilkMic to Applications.
2. Open SilkMic. macOS blocks the first launch because SilkMic is not signed with a paid Apple Developer ID: open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway** (once).
3. Click **Install** when SilkMic asks to install its microphone, and enter your Mac password.
4. Allow microphone access, then pick **SilkMic** as the mic in your call apps.

Requires a Mac with Apple Silicon and macOS 15.4 or later (dictation needs macOS 26).

Check the download: `shasum -a 256 SilkMic-1.0.0.dmg` should match `SilkMic-1.0.0.dmg.sha256`.
